# Pozzo 💊

A personal health app that helps you remember to take your medications through smart reminders.

## 🎯 Purpose

Pozzo's main job-to-be-done is simple: **remind people to take their medications at the right time**.

## ✨ Main Features

- 📷 **Prescription photo upload** — take or upload your medical prescription
- 🔔 **Push notifications** — receive automatic reminders at the right time
- 💾 **Data persistence** — your medications stay saved even after closing the app
- ⏰ **Schedule management** — track which medication to take at which time and day
